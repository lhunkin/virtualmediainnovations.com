01 // Scope

A briefing,
not an encyclopaedia.

HOW TO READ AN ENTRY

Tag — what kind of thing the region is: a bloc, a superpower, a corridor, a frontier. Lede — the entry in one sentence. Body — capability, governance and daily life. Facts — the figures and names worth carrying. Strain — the pressure point.

A WARNING ABOUT CREDENTIALS

The most common cause of a stranded employee is not danger. It is a credential that stops being recognised at a border. Confirm identity, medical authority, augmentation registration and professional licensing separately for every jurisdiction on your route, and do not assume that an Alliance credential travels beyond the Alliance.
