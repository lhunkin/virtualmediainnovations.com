01 // About

A public company with a
private responsibility.

Havelock connects medicine, augmentation, infrastructure, emergency management, artificial intelligence, insurance, logistics, security and strategic recovery inside a single accountable contract chain. When a city loses power, when a hull loses pressure, when a person loses a body — one chain of responsibility answers for the outcome.

“We were not built to prevent disaster. We were built to make sure disaster is survivable, and that somebody is still accountable afterward.”

FOUNDING STATEMENT // REVISED 2071
ORIGIN // FRASER DELTA, 2039

Havelock began as a salvage and pumping contractor working a coastline that three levels of government had already written off. The dikes had failed in sequence over eleven days. The insurers were in litigation. The relief agencies were arguing about jurisdiction.

What the company sold was not equipment. It was a single signature — one organisation that would take the whole failure, end to end, and answer for the result. Fifty-nine years later that is still the only product.

A We take the whole failure

Most contracts fragment a disaster into billable pieces and leave the seams unowned. People die in seams. Havelock contracts are written to cover the entire chain — detection through restoration — with one accountable party at every handoff.

B We plan for the system failing

Every Havelock service is required to have a degraded mode that works without the mesh, without power, and without us. A system that only functions when everything functions is not a continuity system. It is a convenience.

C We file what we did

Every contract closes with a public review. Not a summary — the record. Where we were slow, where we were wrong, and what it cost. Disclosure is scheduled rather than spontaneous, but it is not optional.

PRINCIPLE 01Life before property.

No asset, contract or evidentiary interest outranks a living person. This is not a value statement; it is an operational instruction with a disciplinary consequence.

PRINCIPLE 02Accountability survives the incident.

The obligation does not end when the cameras leave. Restoration and maintenance commitments outlive the news cycle, the contract term, and frequently the client.

PRINCIPLE 03Capability is not ownership.

Havelock may require a person to meet a standard. It may not claim the person who meets it. The Human Capability Charter of 2092 made this binding on the company.

PRINCIPLE 04Somebody has to decide.

Continuity work fails through hesitation more often than through error. Authority is pushed to the person nearest the loss, and that person is named in the record.

03 // Operating Doctrine

The continuity cycle.

Every Havelock contract runs the same five phases, in the same order, with a recorded handoff between each. Phases may overlap. They may not be skipped. The cycle does not close until the review is filed.

01DetectSensor mesh, civil reporting and contract triggers. The clock starts at first credible signal, not at confirmation.
02StabilizeStop the loss. Life before property, always. Nothing is investigated while it is still getting worse.
03RecoverPeople, systems and evidence, in that order. The order is not negotiable and is audited afterward.
04RestoreReturn function to the client and to the public who depend on the client. Both, or the phase is not complete.
05ReviewRecorded, auditable and publicly filed. Including what went wrong and who authorised it.
Life before evidence.Capture before termination.Containment before disclosure.
LIFE BEFORE EVIDENCE

A perfectly preserved scene with a preventable death in it is a failed operation. Personnel are instructed to destroy evidence if that is what it takes to reach someone alive, and are protected from liability when they do.

CAPTURE BEFORE TERMINATION

Havelock field units are equipped and trained to end a threat without ending a person. Lethal force is a failure state with a reporting requirement, not a tactical option with a preference.

CONTAINMENT BEFORE DISCLOSURE

Information about an active hazard is released on a schedule that does not create a second incident. The schedule is recorded in advance and reviewed afterward. Delay is permitted. Silence is not.

RETURN TOGETHER

A dramatic act that leaves the team trapped, exposed or unable to extract is an operational failure regardless of outcome. Personnel are assessed on the state of the whole unit at the end of the task.

COMMON QUESTIONS // PUBLIC ANSWERS
What is the Consent Ledger?

A recorded, per-procedure log of every medical, augmentation and capability intervention performed on you while employed — what was done, who authorised it, what you were told beforehand, and what you agreed to. You hold a copy. It does not stay with the company when you leave, and the company cannot amend an entry without your countersignature.

Can I bring my own lawyer to the agreement review?

Yes, and Havelock will pay for one if you do not have one. Reviewing the agreement with independent counsel is a right under the Human Capability Charter, and declining to exercise it is recorded rather than assumed.

What happens to my augmentations if I leave?

Ordinary restoration and replacement organs are yours and cannot be repossessed. Medically necessary life-support maintenance continues after employment ends — the company cannot abandon it. Company-issued equipment that is not part of your body is returned under the Equipment Responsibility Schedule.

Does Havelock own capabilities I already had?

No. The company may require you to meet a capability standard for a role, and may decline to place you if you do not. It may not claim ownership of a natural body, a naturally occurring power, or a personality. That limit is binding on the company, not discretionary.

What is protected identity, and who can see through it?

Field personnel in certain roles operate under a sealed civic identity with a company-held recovery key. Unsealing requires a recorded request, a named authorising officer and notification to you — except where a court of the North American Alliance orders otherwise, in which case notification is deferred, not cancelled.

How do I report something without going through my supervisor?

The Confidential Reporting Procedure routes to an independent advocate outside your division and outside your chain of command. Reports are logged with a case number you keep. Retaliation is a terminable offence for the retaliating party and is separately reportable.

08 // Human Capability Charter

Your body is not company property.

Ratified 2092 after four years of negotiation with employee representation. The Charter binds Havelock. It is not a policy the company may revise at will, and its articles are enforceable against the company by the individual employee.

ARTICLE IEmployee right

Medical information in plain language, informed consent wherever consent is possible, protected identity where the role requires it, necessary maintenance for as long as it is necessary, independent counsel at company expense, and a recorded Consent Ledger the employee retains.

ARTICLE IIEmployee duty

Disclose capability or equipment changes that could endanger teammates, patients or the public. The duty runs to the person at risk, not to the company, and cannot be waived by a supervisor.

ARTICLE IIICorporate limit

Havelock may require capability standards for a role. It may not claim ownership of a natural body, a naturally occurring power, a personality, or a medically necessary life-support system. No contract term may purport to transfer these.

ARTICLE IVExit protection

Necessary life-support maintenance cannot be abandoned when employment ends, for any reason including dismissal for cause. Ordinary restoration and replacement organs cannot be repossessed, encumbered or used as leverage.

ARTICLE VConsent under duress

Consent obtained during an active incident, under sedation, or where refusal would cost the employee their post is presumed invalid and must be re-confirmed afterward in ordinary conditions. Emergency intervention to save life is permitted and separately recorded.

ARTICLE VIEnforcement

Charter claims go to an independent panel outside the employee's division, with employee representation and a published finding. The company bears the cost of the process regardless of outcome, and may not require confidentiality as a condition of remedy.
